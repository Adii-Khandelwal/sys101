# Accessibility and chrome

Applies to any UI work: components, pages and styles.

## Standing rules

- WCAG AA contrast (lib/contrast.test.ts reads the tokens from app/globals.css in all three blocks: ink, ink-soft and accent on paper and on --skeleton need 4.5:1; ink2 and --edge on paper need 3:1; ink2 is lines, rings and large type only, 3.05:1 light), visible focus rings, full keyboard use, 44px touch targets, real buttons, aria-live for results. Verify at 1440px and 390px.
- Text size: the root is `font-size: 112.5%` (18px at the browser default, so it follows a changed browser text size) and every `font-size` in app/globals.css is in `rem` (px / 18); non-font dimensions stay in px. New type sizes are written in rem.
- Colour scheme: `color-scheme: light` under the light tokens and `dark` under both dark blocks (manual `data-theme` and the OS query), so native controls and scrollbars match. ThemeToggle also rewrites the `theme-color` meta tags to the live `--paper` (and drops their `media`), so the browser bar follows a manual choice. Theme switch: a pointer click reveals the new theme as a circle growing from the click point (the theme reveal, motion.md); a keyboard press swaps instantly; reduced motion uses the existing 80ms fade; a browser without View Transitions swaps instantly.
- Contrast and forced colours: `@media (prefers-contrast: more)` sets `--ink-soft`, `--rule` and `--edge` to `--ink` (tokens only); `@media (forced-colors: active)` gives progress segments, the learn progress track and read boxes a `CanvasText` outline, fills them with `Highlight`, and keeps focus rings.
- Phones: `viewport-fit: cover` (app/layout.tsx) with `padding-inline: env(safe-area-inset-left/right)` on body and the tour tip's bottom inset; every `:hover` rule sits in `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` (focus rules stay outside); fixed-height rails use `dvh`; inputs are 16px or more so iOS does not zoom on focus; `touch-action: manipulation` on buttons, `.cta`, `.text-button`, switches and tabs; `user-select: none` on buttons only; text echoed from the reader (search status and no-result lines, `[aria-live="polite"]`) has `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
- Every feature must work seamlessly on a phone and a laptop: build for both from the start, verify at 390px (touch) and 1440px (keyboard), light and dark, no horizontal overflow, readable wrapping, 44px taps. Wording that names a key, control or view must be true on each screen. A feature is not done until it works on mobile.
- Skeleton loaders match the final size and only stand in for client-loaded parts (no loading.tsx on content routes). Guided tours (Home, topic pages; .claude/rules/tours.md): never auto-start; the link goes once finished or skipped (sys101:tours flag, restorable on /data), a footer link stays; page clicks blocked while open; wording per view (Map, Path, phone); ends at the top; glides by transform.
- Shared header: wordmark and three-node mark linking home, the page's name beside it once its heading scrolls away (faded in from the h1; motion.md), then after a thin rule, lighter, the current section heading (the latest plain `h2` that has passed under the bar, replaced by the next, restored on the way up; decorative, aria-hidden), then the content pages (`SiteNav`: Glossary, Blogs, Great reads, Capstones as small Cabin links (What's new is hidden for now, see news.md) from 900px, a Menu button with a dropdown below; the current page is ink, the menu closes on a page change, Escape or an outside press; the footer keeps the same links), then ThemeToggle. The page does not rubber-band at its ends (`overscroll-behavior-y: none` on html), so the sticky header and its fixed letters stay together. Footer: the site's `LogoMark` (decorative, `aria-hidden`) first, then three plain text columns under a hairline, each with a Cabin sentence-case label (Read: Glossary, Blogs, Great reads, Capstones, Take the tour | The site: About, Terms, Privacy | You: Your data, Report a mistake; REPORT_EMAIL in lib/site.ts); the columns stack on a phone. "Back to top" appears after about 1.5 screens: floating on desktop, in the sticky progress strip on phones.
- About and Terms describe AI assistance with one shared sentence, AI_ASSISTANCE in lib/site.ts (a test checks both pages); never reword it in one place.
- No blue flash on tap: `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` on html; on touch a pressed link turns the accent (--accent on paper, 5.16:1 light, 6.63:1 dark; lib/contrast.test.ts) (`a:active` under `hover: none`), and the focus ring stays. Every list of links that sets its own colour (`color: inherit`) must also set an accent or ink hover, or the global hover is lost (checked by hand with a hover audit).
- Links are not underlined by default (app/globals.css, `a`): a link that stands alone (list, heading, label, footer, button) shows itself by layout and a colour change on hover. Links inside running sentences (`p`, `dd`, `blockquote`) keep an underline in `--ink-soft`, because the accent against ink is 1.98:1 light and 2.14:1 dark, under the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.1 asks for; the footer, `.cta`, labels and `.explain` are excepted. /reads removes every underline (`.reads-page`), the owner's choice for that page.
- Every search or filter box has the homepage search's behaviour: "/" focuses it (not while typing or with Ctrl, Cmd, Alt), Escape clears and blurs, Enter goes to the first result, words match at the start of a word (`matchesAll` in lib/searchMatch.ts), a "/" hint, `aria-keyshortcuts="/"`, an aria-live status. Use `useSlashFocus` and `focusFirstResult` (lib/useSlashFocus.ts). The homepage results list is capped at min(60vh, 480px) and scrolls inside itself (thin scrollbar), so many hits never push the map down.
- Fonts: Libre Caslon Text (400, 700; body, `--font-serif`) and Libre Caslon Display (400; h1 to h3, `--font-display`) are preloaded; Cabin (variable 400-700; labels and UI, `--font-sans`, sentence case, no tracked all-caps) is not (app/layout.tsx). Weights are 400 and 700 only. Italic is a separate, non-preloaded Libre Caslon Text instance, so any rule with `font-style: italic` must also set `font-family: var(--font-serif-italic), Georgia, serif` (otherwise the browser fakes an italic). A test (lib/fonts.test.ts) fails if an italic rule does not name it.
- The left contents rail (`.lesson-rail`, desktop) is capped to the viewport below the header (`max-height`, `overflow-y: auto`, thin scrollbar) so long lists such as the glossary stay reachable; the current row is scrolled into view inside the rail only (LearnRail, LessonRail).
- Scroll ruler (`components/ScrollRuler.tsx`, maths in `lib/scrollRuler.ts`, CSS block "Scroll ruler" near the end of `app/globals.css`): on desktop only (`hover: hover`, `pointer: fine`, width >= 1024px, not `forced-colors`, not `prefers-contrast: more`, hidden in print, and only when the page scrolls more than 40px) a fixed 48px ruler on the right edge replaces the native page scrollbar (`scrollbar-width: none` on `html.has-ruler`, a class an inline script in `app/layout.tsx` adds before first paint when the same media query matches, so a classic scrollbar causes no width jump on hydration; ScrollRuler removes it once measured if the page is too short, and on unmount; no-JS and short pages keep the native bar). It maps the whole page to 0 to 100%: ticks every 2%, 5% and 10% (numbered 10 to 90), a 2px `--ink2` marker with a `--accent` percent readout under it, and a longer 2px `--ink` tick at each h2's position (no names; the lesson rail lists sections). Press or drag scrolls proportionally; a section tick jumps to its heading; never smooth. It is `aria-hidden` and not focusable; wheel, trackpad, keys and touch keep native scrolling. Touch, forced-colours and more-contrast keep the native scrollbar. Body gets 52px right padding from 1024 to 1279px, `.dossier.chapter` gets 52px from 1280px (not both), and "Back to top" sits left of the ruler.
- Text pages (About, Terms, Privacy, Your data) and list pages (Glossary, Background, Great reads, Blogs, 404) use the overprinted page title (`Overprint`, `.page-h1`). About, Terms and Privacy also set their section titles in `Overprint` h2s (`.rail-page`) with `PageRail` (`.page-rail`), a sticky "On this page" list in the left margin from 1200px. Glossary and Background keep the `LearnRail` metro line (styled by the `.glossary-page` and `.background-page` selectors beside `.chapter`); a glide to a section also clears the phone contents strip or a sticky `.jump-line` (`lib/glide.ts`). Privacy and Terms open with a one-sentence "Short version" (`.short-version`) that only restates the page; keep it true when the page changes. Old-style figures (`onum`) were checked and skipped: the loaded Libre Caslon files have no such feature (only frac, liga, tnum).

## Print (topic and learn pages)

- `@media print` in app/globals.css (block "Print" and the chapter additions in it): ink on white, no backgrounds; the header, footer, scroll ruler, lesson rail, back-to-top, tour and "start from zero" links, resume line and quiz score are hidden. The title block prints as one flow (group badge, Topic number, plain title without the overprint copy, Read first with reasons, Makes possible, Time, Full route open), In short framed in a 1pt ink border, margin notes printed in the text flow under their section, and quiz answers open. External source links carry their host in small type from a `data-host` attribute (`hostOf` in lib/site.ts, set on the original post, still-cited and learn source links); other links print as text. Figures, quiz questions, In short, margin notes and route rows do not break across pages.
- Checked with Playwright `emulateMedia({ media: "print" })` screenshots at 816px on two topics and one learn page; dark theme never prints.
