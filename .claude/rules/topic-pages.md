# Topic pages

Applies when editing topic pages, their content JSON or the schema in lib/topics.ts. The build checks most of this. The sections below hold the detail; this list keeps only the rules they do not repeat.

## Standing rules

- A reading-time estimate ("~N min read") shows beside the group label on topic and learn pages and in the All-topics index (not on the homepage start button, which says only "Topic 01"); it is computed from the rendered prose plus a per-diagram allowance (lib/readingTime.ts), never hardcoded.
- A post digest may carry one interactive `figure` (lib/figures.ts, .claude/rules/figures.md): its inputs are digest.numbers rows, labels are HTML outside the SVG, no gradients, filters, idle motion or auto-start; one per post, only where the source gives a mechanism.
- Optional `description` on a topic replaces the generated meta description: one sentence of 8 words or more ending in a period, under 155 characters. Interview tags are hidden for now (SHOW_INTERVIEW_TAGS in lib/topics.ts); the field stays.
- A source with no date (versioned docs) sets `published` to "no date shown" plus `edition`, shown instead of a date only when it gives one the page shows ("date unknown" shows nothing). `tradeoffs.rejected` is left out when the source names no alternative.
- A paper says "This is a research paper, not a company blog post"; no internal phrasing like "the rules" on a page.
- "What's new" (content/news, 3 items max, allowlisted sources) follows What to learn next, untracked, build-checked. No provenance note on the section. The routine touches only content/news/** and docs/news-state.json; read .claude/rules/news.md.
- "Blogs" (/posts, the address stays; the page lists posts, a few papers and docs): every distinct post by topic, company or year (lib/posts.ts); all views are static HTML, ?view= only picks one, canonical /posts, only By topic in Pagefind.
- "Background" (/background, content/background/<slug>.json) lists what a topic's posts assume; concepts taught later get a "Taught later" label and a gist, with no link. Topic pages link to it only if a file exists. Read .claude/rules/background.md.
- Glossary terms in lesson prose (lib/glossaryMatch.ts, components/GlossTerm.tsx): in why, mental model, how, example, mistakes, where they differ and next, the first use per section of each of the topic's own glossary terms (whole word, case-insensitive, optional plural "s", "A / B" and "name (acronym)" split into variants, longest wins, never inside links, headings, code or SVG, text never altered) is an `a.gloss-term` with a dotted underline. Hover (after 300ms for the first popover, at once for a neighbour), focus or tap opens a popover (role dialog, term has aria-describedby on the definition, Escape closes and refocuses the term, flips above and slides sideways to stay in the viewport, opacity and transform only, link 44px on touch); without JavaScript it is a plain link to `/glossary#<topic>-<term-slug>` (the topic is in the id because terms like "replica" repeat). /glossary entries carry that id, get the HighlightTarget wash and a scroll margin below the header; the topic heading above each group is the "taught in" link. The popover is `data-pagefind-ignore` so search never indexes definitions twice.
- Each lesson section heading (why, model, how, example, mistakes, check, differ, exercises) has a "Copy link" button (components/CopyLink.tsx) placed after the h2, not inside it (HeaderTopic only reads an h2 with no child elements). It copies the page address plus the section `#id`, shows "Copied" for 1.6s with a visually hidden status; hidden until hover or focus on a pointer screen, always visible on touch (`hover: none`).

## Specification

- Order: Part A concept (Why it exists, Mental model, How it works, Worked example, Common mistakes, Check yourself), Part B real world (one section per post, then Where they differ), then Design exercises, What to learn next, Original posts. Each section opens with one line "After this you can ...". A "Skip to the posts" link sits at the top, with one quiet "Start from zero" text link beside it to the learn page. The Original posts section stays.
- Concept text may come from general knowledge, but every figure or company claim must come from a verified post in the JSON. Never invent numbers.
- Check yourself: 5 to 8 questions. Each answer explains why it is right and why the others are wrong, and links back to the teaching section. Option order lives in the content: spread the right answer across positions (none over half a topic's questions, no more than 2 in a row; the build fails on all in one position, a run of 4+, more than half in one position (4 or more questions), or a letter like "B" in an explanation), with "Neither" or "All of the above" last. Explanations quote option text, never letters. The build also fails when the correct positions repeat in a cycle of 2 or 3 (such as A,B,C,A,B,C) or when the correct option is the single longest in more than 2 questions (lib/topics.ts checkQuizStyle; QUIZ_STYLE_PENDING lists topics not yet reworked). Saved answers are keyed by option id (lib/quizIds.ts), so reordering is safe; lib/legacyQuiz.ts is the frozen v2 order used to upgrade old saves and is never edited. "Where they differ" is 2 to 3 short paragraphs (what they share, where they diverge, the evidence); the build checks it. Design exercises: 2 to 3 in rising difficulty (apply, design, scale 10x) with model answers hidden until clicked.
- Diagrams are original inline SVG in the site style. Never copy or embed a blog's diagram. Link to an original figure only if verified it exists. Every diagram has a title and alt text. A topic may add an optional `lesson.why.diagram` (shown under Why it exists; counted in reading time). A topic may add an optional `lesson.mistakesDiagram` (under Common mistakes) and an optional `diagram` on each exercise (inside the revealed model answer); both are counted in reading time. A topic may add an optional `comparisonDiagram` (under Where they differ; counted in reading time), a side-by-side of the posts using only what the comparison and digests say. A post digest may add an optional `chart` (bar chart under the numbers table): only for two or more `numbers` that share a unit, bar lengths proportional to the values, and the build rejects any figure in its text that is not in `numbers`. A glossary entry may add an optional `diagram` for a term that is really a shape or a process; it shows on /glossary only, never in the popover or the topic page. `lesson.howItWorks.stepper` is an optional step-through (components/StepThrough.tsx): 3 to 6 `{ caption, diagram }` frames of one diagram at one shared viewBox with unique ids, shown one at a time with Back, Next step, Reset and the arrow keys (no motion; the no-JavaScript fallback lists every frame). Frames use the same diagram rules and only what the How it works steps say. The build requires a diagram on the Mental model, How it works and Worked example concept sections, and on every post digest.
- Original posts lists each source with company and year (just the company for a source that shows no date). The page carries one "Page last updated" line at the bottom, not a date per source. Links open in a new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer". Missing attribution fields fail the build.
- No data controls on topic pages. They live on /data.
- Optional `interviewRelevance` on a topic ("core", "common" or "niche", in lib/topics.ts) shows a small Cabin label marker beside the group label (components/InterviewTag.tsx), state carried by the text with only "core" taking the accent. The build rejects any other value; a topic without the field shows no marker.

## In short and edge reasons

- `summary`: `points` (3 to 5 sentences, 8 to 28 words each, digits only if already in the topic JSON, no em dash or "it's not X" phrasing) and `figures` (0 to 3 of `{postId, row, label}`: the row is in a post's `digest.numbers`, the label is a hand-written plain phrase of 4 to 15 words with no editorial wording, every number in it appearing in that row and the row's value in it). Rendered as "In short" under the concept; a figure reads "<label>, <Company> <date string>", the date string being exactly what the post header shows (an undated source shows its edition label), plus "(company's own figure)" added automatically when the row's meaning calls it a headline or a target. Each figure links to its post section. Not tracked, not in the rail, indexed by search.
- `prerequisiteReasons`: `{ [prerequisiteId]: sentence }` for the full prerequisite set, 8 to 30 words, no digits. Shown under each Read first link (the right column of the title block), in Path rows and in the learn page Before you start. Not on the map tooltip (see docs/tasks/batch-1.md).
- Write both only through drafts files (`scripts/topic-brief.mjs`, `scripts/apply-drafts.mjs`). Restate only what the page already says.
- Style: one unit format everywhere ("500 ms", "100 TB", "8 million", never "500ms"), and a figure label is a phrase, never "label: value". A label may use numbers from its row, from another row of the same post, or the post year (for example "Postgres 9.2 ... (2016 account)").

## Page last updated

- Every content page ends with one line, "Page last updated <date>", from the `PageUpdated` component, just above the footer. It replaces the per-source "Checked on" dates, the "As of" line under What's new, and the "Last updated" label that used to sit at the top of Terms and Privacy.
- `verifiedOn` is the date that entry was last edited; no code writes it. Never imply the author personally checked sources unless true.
- The date is derived, never typed on the page: a topic page uses its newest post `verifiedOn` or its What's new `asOf` (`topicPageUpdated` in lib/site.ts); a learn page its newest source `verifiedOn` (`learnUpdated`); /background the newest `updatedOn`; About, Glossary, Terms and Privacy use `PAGE_DATES`. The same dates feed the sitemap lastmod.
- A source that shows no date shows no date: "no date shown" and "date unknown" never appear on a page (`postDateShown`, `companyWhen`). An `edition` label that gives a date the page shows ("page shows last modified June 14, 2021, v3.5 documentation") is still shown.
- lib/pageUpdated.test.ts renders every topic, learn page and the five other pages and checks all of this.

## Chapter layout (Phase 5 of the redesign; topic and learn pages)

- Class `.dossier.chapter` (max 1360px). Title block (`.chapter-head`): group badge (`.chapter-group`, a 1.5px ink frame), "Topic NN" (reading-order number from `lib/order.ts`, topic pages only; the learn page shows "Start from zero" instead), the overprinted h1 (`components/Overprint.tsx`, `.chapter-h1`, `clamp(2.4rem, 7.2vw, 6.4rem)`; the h1 is one text child; learn pages use the smaller `.is-learn`), and a right column `dl.chapter-meta`: "Read first" (prerequisite links with their reasons), "Makes possible" (dependents) and "Time" (the one place "~N min read" shows). On a learn page the column is "Back to <topic>" and "Time". Every section h2 (topic and learn) is an `Overprint`.
- Order inside the text column: resume line, "In short" (a 1.5px ink frame on `--skeleton`, label inside at the top), the concept paragraph, the Start from zero / Skip to the posts / tour links, then Part A and Part B.
- From 1200px one grid (`.chapter-grid`, columns 190px, 700px, 250px; `chapter-head`, `chapter-body` and `chapter-main` are `display: contents`): row 1 is the badge and title (starting about 40px under the header, columns 1 and 2), row 2 is the lesson rail and `.chapter-intro` (In short, concept, links), and the right column (Read first, Makes possible, Time) is top-aligned and spans rows 1 and 2, so a long list of links never adds space above or below the title. Below 1200px it is one column: title, then the right column as a normal block. The margin column (250px) is `position: absolute` beside each section, with a sticky inner box. Below 1200px the rail is the sticky strip, the margin is `display: none`. 1180 to 1199px also uses the strip.
- Rail as a metro line (`LessonRail`, `LearnRail`; `CapstoneRail` uses the same `.station`; `.rail-marker` is no longer used by any component): a 3px ink vertical line with a `.station` per row. State is shape: done is a filled disc, not yet done is hollow, the current row has a ring round it and a `--ink2` dot. The count ("x of N done"), segmented bar, Continue link and "Mark done" are unchanged; "Mark done" uses a round station.
- Route to this topic (`lib/route.ts` `routeTo`, `components/RouteToTopic.tsx`): in the right column of a lesson topic, after Time, a collapsed `<details>` (works without JavaScript) listing every prerequisite, transitive, in an order that never puts a topic before one it builds on (depth-first over the listed prerequisites, each once; nothing is shown for a topic without prerequisites). Each row is a link, its reading time (`readingMinutes` with that topic's news, the same figure as its own page) and a read mark by shape (hollow ring unread, filled disc read, from the existing read list, with hidden text for screen readers). The route total (sum of those times, the topic's own time excluded) appears once, inside it. No new data; test in `lib/route.test.ts`.
- Margin column (`components/MarginTerms.tsx`, `sectionTerms` and `noteId` in `lib/glossaryMatch.ts`, `stackTops` in `lib/marginStack.ts`): every section with inline popovers (why, model, how, example, mistakes, differ, next) shows every glossary entry whose popover it holds (first use per section, no cap, no counts) with the entry's definition, one source for both. Notes are numbered 1, 2, 3 in order of first use, restarting in each section: the note starts with a small superscript number (`.margin-num`) and the term in the prose carries the same number from 1200px as CSS generated content on `.gloss[data-num]::after` with an empty alt (`content: attr(data-num) / ""`), so it is not in the text, find on page, search or the accessibility tree and adds no layout shift. It is an `aria-hidden` copy (the popovers stay the accessible path at every width), `data-pagefind-ignore`, skipped by `lib/findOnPage.ts` (`.margin-terms` in `SKIPPED`). From 1200px, after layout, each note is placed beside the line that holds its term (`data-note` on the term, `data-note-for` on the note, set from the term's top inside the section), pushed down so none overlap (8px gap), pulled up to stay inside the section, and the section grows (`min-height`) when they cannot fit, so notes of two sections never meet; no animation; ResizeObserver and font load re-place. Without JavaScript, or when a term is not found, the notes stay a sticky list under the section label. Hovering or focusing a term marks its note with a 1.5px ink left edge and the term with the highlight wash, and the reverse (`data-linked`, a small handler on the section). The 48px scroll ruler is cleared by body padding of 52px from 1024 to 1279px and by `.dossier.chapter` padding of 52px from 1280px (never both). Notes are Cabin 14.5px, `--ink-soft` definitions. A learn page's margin lists each stage's official reading as plain text (title, publisher, year; no links), unplaced. No source line or figure caption goes in the margin: the post header already carries the company and date, and figures have no caption data.
- Headings reworded in this phase: "Builds on" is "Read first", "Leads to" is "Makes possible". Tracked section ids and names (`LESSON_OUTLINE`, tours, anchors) are unchanged.
- Sub-headings inside a post (Constraints, Walkthrough ...) and the "After this you can" lines are Cabin, not italic.
