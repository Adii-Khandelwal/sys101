# Motion

Applies to any CSS or component animation.

## Standing rules

- One tokens file (lib/motion.ts, mirrored in app/globals.css and checked by lib/motion.test.ts): 120ms fast, 200ms base, 320ms slow, plus `--dur-page` 160ms (the page cross-fade out), `--dur-pulse` 1400ms (skeleton pulse, breathing, the search-jump highlight hold) and `--dur-reduced` 80ms (the fade that survives reduced motion). Ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) for entering or responding, ease-in-out for on-screen movement.
- Animate only transform, opacity, and color. The one named layout exception is `.reveal` (components/Reveal.tsx: quiz explanations, "Show" panels, exercise answers), which animates `grid-template-rows` 0fr to 1fr over `--dur-base` with ease-out while its content fades and rises 6px, so the space is reserved and nothing jumps; keep it to small disclosure content, never page-level layout. No bounce or elastic, no layout shift, no staggered or scroll-triggered effects. One exception: the homepage heading's letters fly into the top bar ("sys101" into the wordmark), one letter after another, driven by scroll position over 120px and eased (the shown progress follows the scroll position with a 70ms time constant, so a wheel jump is not a letter jump; layout is measured once and on resize or font load, never per frame) (lib/letterRise.ts: transform only, translate and scale about each letter's centre, requestAnimationFrame, passive listener). The heading is split into letters at runtime and restored on cleanup; the header's own letters are invisible targets; each letter is pinned with position: fixed the moment it arrives, not when the easing finishes (re-aiming a letter that scrolls with the page jitters, and the easing tail would show it), and a letter whose target lies past the header's right edge fades out instead. Reduced motion: the name is simply there or not. Press feedback is `scale(0.97)` on `:active` for buttons, `.text-button`, the lesson-top and progress links, the header nav and menu links, `.path-title`, and (on the `li`, so the stretched link keeps its hit area) search result rows and the left rail rows; the `.cta` and Menu button have their own. Reduced motion removes the scale from all of them.
- Every other page: no letters fly. The page name (h1) and the current section name (the latest plain serif `h2`, not a Cabin `.label` one, that has passed under the bar) fade into the top bar with the scroll over the same 120px (lib/headerFade.ts, HeaderTopic: opacity and 6px of translateY only). The next section heading fades the previous one out as it arrives and the previous one fades back as you scroll up; reduced motion switches at the halfway point. The page name has no rule before it; at the very end of a page, headings too close to the end to reach the bar count as arrived at the last scroll position (`fadeProgress`), so only the last shows; a thin rule sits between it and the section name, which is lighter (smaller, `--ink-soft`) than the page name (`--ink`).
- Opening screen (components/Splash.tsx, `.splash` in app/globals.css): shown on the first load of the homepage in a browser session (an inline script sets a `sys101:splash` flag in sessionStorage as it starts it; a refresh, or a later visit in the same tab, does not replay it; a storage error in the script means no splash) and on no other page. One node of the mark (the right one) comes on like a bulb, then the other two come out of it into place (`splash-out-up`, `splash-out-down`: transform only, `--flicker-out` 500ms), flickering, while the name "sys101" does the same: on, dim, off, dim, on and staying on (`splash-bulb`, opacity only, `--flicker` 900ms each; the single node at 120ms, the two at 520ms, the name at 520ms; the lines fade in at 1000ms), a short rest, then the mark and the name fly, each scaled to its own target, onto the masthead's own mark and "sys101" (transform only, `--dur-slow` 320ms, ease-in-out; the masthead lockup is hidden with `.is-landing` until they land, and the hand-over happens on `transitionend`) while the paper backdrop fades out over the same 320ms (about 2.3s in all). If the masthead is not on screen, the backdrop only fades. The no-script fallback fade waits `--splash-hold` plus the flight. It only covers a page that is already in the HTML, so LCP and no-JavaScript are untouched: an inline script in the layout sets `data-splash` on `<html>` before paint, and runs only when the path is `/`, and not for reduced motion or automation (`navigator.webdriver`, so the checks never wait for it). Going to the homepage by a link is not a load, so it does not play; opening any other page directly does not play it either. A click or any key skips it. An exception to "never fade in the page": the page is not faded in, an overlay is faded out.
- Glossary popover (components/GlossTerm.tsx, `.gloss-pop`): opacity and a 0.97 scale from the term (`transform-origin` at the term's left edge, top or bottom as it opens down or up), `--dur-fast` in and two thirds of that out; the closed state collapses its width only after the fade-out so the exit is seen. Hover: the first popover opens after 300ms (a mouse sweeping across prose opens none); a neighbour, or any popover opened within 300ms of one closing, opens at once with no animation (`data-instant`). Focus and click open at once.
- Phone menu (`.site-nav-menu`): fades in over `--dur-fast` with a 6px rise (`menu-in`); no exit animation (it unmounts).
- The homepage start button (NextUp) focuses the topic's map node and opens the page at once for a keyboard click; a pointer click waits one `--dur-base` on that node first. Never animate or delay a keyboard-started action.
- Slow navigation (components/NavPending.tsx, `.nav-pending`): a click on a link to another page that has not arrived after 400ms shows the mark, breathing (`bubble-breathe`), over the page until the new path arrives (10s limit; instant under reduced motion). A Map node click is not a link click and keeps its own bubble (graph.md), and nothing shows while the opening screen is up.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing moves, but a fade that helps you follow a change stays as a short opacity fade: `--dur-fast` becomes `--dur-reduced` (80ms), so tooltips, popovers, swaps, the phone menu, quiz reveals, the tour box and the next question fade in 80ms; `--dur-base`, `--dur-slow` and `--dur-page` are 0ms, and transforms (press scale, popover scale, content rise, breathing) are removed. Never fade in the page itself on load: it delays LCP. The one exception is the Map node-open transition (.claude/rules/graph.md): a fade-in after a plain click or tap on a Map node, never on a direct load.
- The bar shows no section name on About, Terms, Privacy and Your data (NO_SECTIONS in components/HeaderTopic.tsx), only the page name.
- Links to a section glide: a link to a `#section` on the same page scrolls down to it eased (lib/glide.ts, 320ms up to 700ms, stopped by touch, wheel or a key); a link to a section on another page opens that page at its top and then glides down (components/ViewTransitions.tsx, `navigateWithTransition`; Next is told not to jump). Reduced motion jumps. Opening an address that already has a `#section` is left to the browser.
