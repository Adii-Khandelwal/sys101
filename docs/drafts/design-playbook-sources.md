## Dropped (not confirmed on a raw page, or failed a rule)

1. Jeff Dean talk year and venue: the extracted PDF text gives no date. Only PDF metadata (created 17 Mar 2009) was seen. The file name says "stanford-295-talk"; do not state a year other than "PDF dated 2009" until a dated source is opened.
2. Dean's authorship of the jboner gist numbers: only the gist's own credit line says so.
3. The 2026 LLM rows in the jboner gist (local LLM generate 1 token 15 ms, frontier LLM time to first token 1,000 ms, and the rest): present on the page but written by a gist contributor with no measurement or method; no engineering source. Dropped.
4. Barroso and Holzle, "The Datacenter as a Computer" (Morgan and Claypool, doi 10.2200/S00516ED2V01Y201306CAC024): the curl fetch returned no response (HTTP 000), so no figure was confirmed. Not used.
5. Any "requests per second per server", "storage per user" or "QPS" rule of thumb: no primary source opened. Not added.
6. Interview structure steps (requirements, estimates, API, data model, high-level design, deep dive, bottlenecks) as taught by Xu, Grokking and Educative: paid sources, excluded. Titles may serve only as a checklist.
7. Dean slide year-1 hardware failures reflect a 2009 cluster; keep only with the year, or drop for a page about current hardware.
