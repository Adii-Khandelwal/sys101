# Graph specification

Moved unchanged from CLAUDE.md. Read before editing graph code.

- The graph is the homepage on desktop (768px and up). Under 768px the homepage shows only the Path view (components/PathView.tsx), a linear grouped syllabus, with no toggle; on desktop a Map/Path toggle switches between them and the choice is remembered per browser. Layout is deterministic, left to right by prerequisite depth, and panning never moves nodes.
- Under INTERACTIVE_MIN_NODES topics (12, in lib/graphUi.ts), or on a phone (under 768px) at any count, the map is fixed and fitted: no zoom, pan, or map controls, and clicking a node cross-fades to its topic. A phone always gets this fixed map so a finger scrolls the page past it and a tap opens the topic; zoom and pan are desktop-only. At 768px and up, from INTERACTIVE_MIN_NODES topics the zoom/pan/Fit-all controls switch on. On desktop the map is always a full-size horizontal graph (labels at full size, never shrunk or stacked): GraphView lays it out across the width its labels need, and the stage renders at that content width inside a horizontally-scrolling wrapper (components, `.graph-scrollwrap`), so when the map is wider than the viewport the whole map section scrolls sideways with a real scrollbar. The viewport width is measured on the wrapper and the stage width is driven by the computed content width, so they never feed back on each other. The map is static: a node click just highlights briefly and cross-fades (no camera recentre). A phone stacks vertically, one topic per row. After adding a topic, check the map at 1280px, 1440px, and 390px for zero crossings and overlaps. Group labels sit directly above their first node with their left edge on the node's, else below it the same way; stacked, they sit right of the node's label; never pinned to the stage edge.
- One fitRatio, recomputed on load, resize, and node-set change. Zoom range 0.6x to 8x of the fitted view, with identical limits for wheel, pinch, buttons, keys, and double-click. Ctrl or Cmd plus wheel zooms and plain wheel scrolls the page. Fit all always restores the identical state. 5px drag threshold. 64px pan limit.
- Draw only the transitive reduction of prerequisites by default. Hidden edges show faintly on hover or focus, and with "Show all links" once the map is interactive. Data and build checks use the full set.
- Node states that do not rely on color: unread (thin outline), ready (all prerequisites read, thicker outline), read (filled rust). Not-yet nodes stay clickable.
- No edge crosses a label or node. Labels sit right of nodes with a 16px gap. Tooltips are viewport-clamped and never clipped. Unrelated items fade to no lower than 60% opacity, and highlight always clears.
- Group labels are real buttons: clicking one highlights just that group's topics (its nodes stay opaque, the rest fade to no lower than 60%, like a node highlight), with the clicked label taking the rust accent and aria-pressed. Clicking it again, Escape, a scroll, or a click outside clears it; hovering or focusing a node takes over. Computed by groupSet in lib/graphUi.ts.
- Keyboard traversal, aria-live announcements, 44px hit areas, and a plain text index as the full fallback, including when WebGL fails. Smooth at 150 nodes.
- Pivot trigger: when the graph stops being readable (crowded nodes or unreadable edges), the Path view is the fallback. The Path view and the mobile Path default under 768px are built; see docs/ideas.md ideas 5 and 6. (The separate zoom/pan interactivity threshold is INTERACTIVE_MIN_NODES, currently 12; see above.)
- Touch: an interactive map is desktop-only; there one finger pans (the drag follows the finger) and two fingers pinch to zoom. On a phone the map is always fixed and non-interactive, so it captures no touch, a finger scrolls the page past it, a tap opens the topic, and pull to refresh works.

## Node-open transition (the one exception to the page fade-in rule in CLAUDE.md)

- A plain pointer click or touch tap on a Map node grows its bubble, then the topic page opens with a short fade-in: 200ms (`--dur-base`) of growth, then a 200ms opacity-only fade, about 400ms. Both durations come from the tokens.
- The bubble is a DOM copy of the node (ring, fill, centre dot, same colours) placed over it and scaled by `transform` to 2.4x (`BUBBLE_SCALE`): compositor only, no layout, and it starts exactly over the node so nothing flashes. The fade-in is the View Transitions cross-fade with `html[data-nav="node-open"]`: the old page stays put, the new one fades in (`vt-fade-in`, opacity only). `onSwap` removes the bubble when the new page is in the DOM, so it is not in the new snapshot. Without the API the topic simply opens after the growth.
- Plays only from `clickNode` in components/GraphView.tsx. Path view, search, the All-topics index and direct links never import it, and keep the ordinary page cross-fade.
- `clickIntent` (lib/nodeOpen.ts) decides: Ctrl, Cmd, Shift or Alt held, or a middle click, opens a new tab with no animation; reduced motion, or a click with detail 0 (script or assistive technology), opens at once; a keyboard Enter on a node's link is the plain link, untouched. A double click, or a second click, cancels the bubble.
- Back keeps the view: when a node is opened the camera and the sideways scroll are saved to sessionStorage (lib/mapView.ts). The Map restores them once if it mounts right after a popstate (Back or Forward) and the data is under 30 minutes old; any other visit starts fitted. The bubble is removed when the Map unmounts and on `pageshow` from the back/forward cache, so none can be left behind.
- The Map is not shown under 768px (the Path view is), so the animation is for pointer and touch devices at 768px and up.
